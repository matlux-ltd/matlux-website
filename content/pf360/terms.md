This page is hosted on the Matlux Ltd website for convenience. The application is not a public Matlux Ltd service.

Questions may be sent to [mathieu@matlux.net](mailto:mathieu@matlux.net).

## Purpose

The application retrieves information from payment accounts explicitly connected by its operator and uses that information for personal net-worth, cash, balance and transaction reporting.

It does not provide regulated financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, credit decisions or payment-initiation services. Reports and calculations are informational and may require independent verification.

## Account authorisation

Every account connection must be authorised by the account holder using the authentication and consent process provided by the relevant bank and Enable Banking. The application must not be used to access an account without proper authority.

The account holder remains responsible for reviewing active consents and revoking access that is no longer wanted.

The application may be affected by bank outages, consent expiry, strong-authentication requirements, incomplete transaction history, delayed balances or changes to third-party APIs.

## Accuracy and availability

The application is maintained on a reasonable-efforts basis for private use. No guarantee is made that data will always be complete, current or error-free. Important financial, tax, legal or investment decisions should be checked against authoritative bank statements and appropriate professional advice.

## Security

API keys, tokens and exported financial data are confidential. They must not be disclosed or made available to unauthorised people. If credentials or a connected account may have been compromised, the relevant access should be revoked and replaced promptly.
