## Who operates PF360 Personal Net Worth

PF360 Personal Net Worth is a private, non-commercial personal-finance application operated by **Mathieu Serge Nicolas Gauthron**, acting in his personal capacity.

This notice is hosted on the Matlux Ltd website for convenience. The application is not a public Matlux Ltd service and is not offered to customers or other third parties.

For privacy questions, contact [mathieu@matlux.net](mailto:mathieu@matlux.net).

## What the application does

The application allows its operator to retrieve and analyse information from payment accounts that he owns and has explicitly connected through Enable Banking. Its purpose is personal net-worth, liquidity, balance and transaction reporting.

## Information processed

Depending on what a connected bank makes available, the application may process:

- account identifiers and account details;
- account balances and currencies;
- transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, references and counterparty details;
- bank-consent, connection and synchronisation metadata; and
- reports and calculations derived from that information.

The application does not ask for or store online-banking passwords, PINs or strong-authentication codes. Authentication is completed directly with the relevant bank or its authorised authentication service.

## How information is obtained and used

Account information is obtained only after the account holder has deliberately authorised access through the relevant bank. It is used only to maintain the operator's private financial records, produce personal reports and monitor his financial position.

The information is not sold, used for advertising or made available as a service to other people.

## Service providers and banks

Enable Banking Oy provides the regulated open-banking connection used by the application. The relevant bank and Enable Banking process information as necessary to establish and maintain an authorised connection. Their own privacy notices apply to their processing.

Information may also be processed by infrastructure or storage providers selected by the operator solely to run, secure or back up the application. Access is limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

## Retention and security

Financial records may be retained for as long as they remain useful for the operator's personal financial history and reporting. Connection and consent records may be retained while a connection remains active and for a reasonable period afterwards for security and troubleshooting.

The operator uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of financial information. API private keys and access credentials must be kept confidential and separate from exported reporting data.

## Control over connected accounts

Access to an account can be revoked through the relevant bank, through Enable Banking's consent-management interface where available, or by contacting the operator. Revoking future access does not automatically erase information already retrieved and retained in the operator's private records.

## Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the application, its providers or its data-handling practices change. The current version and revision date will remain available at this URL.
