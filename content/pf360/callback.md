## Your bank has returned you to PF360

Leave this tab open while the local PF360 process completes the connection. You can close it after PF360 confirms that the account has been connected.

No banking password, PIN or strong-authentication code should be entered on this page. Authentication takes place only on your bank's own pages or its authorised authentication service.

If you reached this page without deliberately connecting an account, close the tab and contact [mathieu@matlux.net](mailto:mathieu@matlux.net).
